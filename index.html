About Evans Solar Energy

Evans Solar Energy is a solar equipment dealer located at Alaba International Market, Ojo, Lagos State, Nigeria. We offer a range of solar energy products and accessories for customers looking for reliable equipment for residential, commercial, and other energy applications.

Our product range includes solar panels, hybrid inverters, lithium-ion batteries, tubular batteries, solar street lights, solar fans, charge controllers, solar security cameras, portable solar power stations, floor lights, and other solar accessories.

Our goal is to make it easier for customers to discover and purchase the solar products that suit their energy needs.
